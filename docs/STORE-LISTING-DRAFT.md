## Full description

StreamioX is a controller-first media discovery and playback client designed for the Xbox living-room experience.

Browse movie and series catalogs, explore available genres, search catalogs that support search, and manage compatible add-ons from one interface built for television screens and game controllers.

### Explore
- Browse featured titles and popular movie and series catalogs.
- Discover titles through available genre and catalog sections.
- View title details before choosing what to watch.

### Search
- Search across installed catalog add-ons that advertise search support.
- Review matching movie and series results in an Xbox-friendly interface.

### Manage add-ons
- Add compatible add-ons using their manifest URL.
- Synchronize installed add-ons and review their available catalog resources.
- Remove add-ons you no longer use.

### Your library
- Browse library entries with movie and series filters.
- Sort entries by recently watched, title, or recently added.

### Living-room playback
- Use a dedicated player interface with playback, seeking and playback-position controls.
- Playback availability and supported formats depend on the selected stream and provider.

StreamioX is an independent third-party client. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Stremio. Add-ons and external providers are operated by their respective developers and may have separate terms, privacy practices and content availability. Users are responsible for choosing lawful sources and ensuring that their use complies with applicable laws.

StreamioX does not provide, host, or guarantee the availability of third-party media streams.
