# StreamioX Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026

## 1. Who we are
StreamioX is an independent application published by Kiwi Cloud. This policy explains the information the app handles when you use it.

## 2. Information stored on your Xbox
StreamioX stores some app data locally on your device so that features can work between app sessions. Depending on the features you use, this may include:
- Your Stremio authentication key, stored using the Windows PasswordVault credential facility.
- Stremio account profile information, stored in the app's local settings.
- Installed add-on details, stored in the app's local app data.
- Your saved library entries and playback progress, stored in a local library file.

This information is stored on your device. The app's local storage is not the same as a cloud account or a server operated by the StreamioX publisher.

You can sign out of your Stremio account in the app. Local library and add-on data may remain on the device until removed by the app or the app's data is reset/uninstalled. Avoid using the app on a device shared with people you do not trust.

## 3. Information sent to third-party services
When you use online features, StreamioX communicates with Stremio services and the compatible add-ons you install or use. Depending on the action, requests may include account authentication information, add-on configuration, title identifiers, catalog or metadata queries, and stream requests. Network providers may also receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address.

Stremio and third-party add-on operators process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Kiwi Cloud does not control their systems, retention practices or security. Review the policies of the services and add-ons you choose to use.

## 4. Publisher-operated analytics and advertising
The publisher has indicated that StreamioX does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. The app is not intended to send usage analytics or crash reports to the publisher.

## 5. Data sharing
The publisher does not operate a StreamioX user account database or app backend for the features described here. Information is sent to Stremio and compatible third-party services when required for the feature you choose to use. Those providers may process the information they receive.

## 6. Data security
StreamioX uses the Windows PasswordVault facility for the saved Stremio authentication key. Other app data is stored in the app's local storage. No method of storage or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device and account credentials protected.

## 7. Children's privacy
StreamioX is a general-purpose client and does not knowingly collect children's personal information for the publisher. Third-party add-ons may expose content or services that are not suitable for all ages. Parents and guardians should supervise use where appropriate.

## 8. Changes
This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at the public policy URL supplied in the Microsoft Store listing.
