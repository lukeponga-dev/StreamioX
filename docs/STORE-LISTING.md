## Full description
StreamioX brings a controller-focused Stremio-compatible experience to Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S.

Browse movie and series catalogs, explore title details, search supported catalogs, manage compatible add-ons, and access your saved library from a TV-friendly interface designed for Xbox controllers.

Sign in with your Stremio account to sync supported account add-ons. StreamioX connects to Stremio services and compatible third-party add-ons to retrieve catalog information, metadata and available stream sources. Playback availability and content depend on the add-ons and services you choose to configure.

Features:
- Xbox controller-first navigation and TV-friendly layout.
- Browse movie and series catalogs provided by compatible add-ons.
- View title details and metadata supplied by providers.
- Search catalogs that advertise search support.
- Install and manage Stremio-compatible add-ons.
- Sign in to Stremio and synchronise supported account add-ons.
- Save titles to a local library and track playback progress.
- Play compatible streams using the app's playback engine.

Important information:
- StreamioX is an independent third-party client and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Stremio.
- StreamioX does not provide, host, or own movies, television programmes, or streaming sources.
- Content, stream availability, provider reliability and add-on behaviour are controlled by the third-party services and add-ons you choose to use.
- You are responsible for ensuring that your use of third-party services and content complies with applicable laws and provider terms.
- A Stremio account and compatible add-ons may be required for some features.
- Internet connection is required for online catalogs, account synchronisation and streaming.
